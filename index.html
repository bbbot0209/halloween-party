Ideas board

Decor

  • Swap porch + lamp bulbs for pink ones
  • A swarm of paper bats flying up one wall
  • Stretchy pink cobwebs on the mantel and doorways
  • Velvet pumpkin clusters as centerpieces
  • Tinsel curtain photo wall by the entry
  • Ghost swirls hanging from the ceiling
  • Flameless tea lights in every window

Games + activities

  • Pumpkin painting bar — pinks, black, gold
  • Costume contest: scariest, prettiest, best duo
  • Horror trivia face-off in teams
  • Pin the eyeball on the zombie (blindfolded)
  • Glow-stick dance break at 10pm
  • Classic horror movie playing silently on loop

Costume ideas

  • Zombie prom queen in a pink gown
  • Pink Ladies from Grease — as ghosts
  • Vampire in rose satin + fangs
  • Corpse bride in blush tulle
  • Barbie & Ken, horror-movie edition
  • Pink Power Ranger

Playlist vibe

  • Campy classics: “Monster Mash,” “Thriller”
  • Retro eerie: “Spooky” — Dusty Springfield
  • “Season of the Witch” — Donovan
  • Pop drama: “Bad Romance” — Lady Gaga
  • “Heads Will Roll” — Yeah Yeah Yeahs
  • “Pink Venom” — BLACKPINK
