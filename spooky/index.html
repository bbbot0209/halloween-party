Pick a theme

Haunted Manor

Dust sheets over the furniture, cobwebbed candelabras, crooked portraits, fog rolling across the floor.

Abandoned Carnival

Flickering bulbs, a rusted ticket booth, creepy clowns, and caramel apples nobody should trust.

Witches’ Coven

Bubbling cauldron punch, potion bottles, dried herbs, black candles, and pointed hats required.

Victorian Séance

Mourning black, lace veils, a candlelit table, and a spirit board for midnight — the one pink touch: faded roses.

Graveyard Shift

Tombstones by the door, a skeleton bartender, moss, bones, and a dirt-cup dessert bar.

Ideas board

Decor

  • Swap lamp + porch bulbs for flickering flame bulbs
  • A swarm of black bats flying up one wall
  • Stretchy white cobwebs on the mantel and doorways
  • Creepy cloth draped over mirrors, frames, and furniture
  • A life-size skeleton greeting guests at the door
  • Low fog across the floor right as people arrive
  • Black taper candles on every surface

Games + activities

  • Pumpkin carving bar — black, orange, bone paint for no-carve
  • Costume contest: scariest, most cursed, best duo
  • Horror trivia face-off in teams
  • Pin the eyeball on the zombie (blindfolded)
  • Lights-out ghost story at midnight
  • Classic horror movie playing silently on loop

Costume ideas

  • Plague doctor, beak mask and all
  • Morticia + Gomez Addams
  • Victorian ghost bride in tattered lace
  • A coven of three witches
  • Michael Myers, Ghostface, or Pennywise
  • Vampire in black velvet + fangs

Playlist vibe

  • Campy classics: “Monster Mash,” “Thriller”
  • “I Put a Spell on You” — Screamin’ Jay Hawkins
  • “Season of the Witch” — Donovan
  • “Somebody’s Watching Me” — Rockwell
  • “Heads Will Roll” — Yeah Yeah Yeahs
  • “This Is Halloween” — The Nightmare Before Christmas
