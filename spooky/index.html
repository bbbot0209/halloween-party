Ideas board

Decor

  • Swap lamp + porch bulbs for flickering flame bulbs
  • A swarm of black bats flying up one wall
  • Stretchy white cobwebs on the mantel and doorways
  • Creepy cloth draped over mirrors, frames, and furniture
  • A life-size skeleton greeting guests at the door
  • Low fog across the floor right as people arrive
  • Black taper candles on every surface

Games + activities

  • Pumpkin carving bar — black, orange, bone paint for no-carve
  • Horror trivia face-off in teams
  • Pin the eyeball on the zombie (blindfolded)
  • Lights-out ghost story at midnight
  • Classic horror movie playing silently on loop

Costume ideas

  • Plague doctor, beak mask and all
  • Morticia + Gomez Addams
  • Victorian ghost bride in tattered lace
  • A coven of three witches
  • Michael Myers, Ghostface, or Pennywise
  • Vampire in black velvet + fangs
